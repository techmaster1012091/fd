The concept

A better way to plan the trip.

FleetSignal explores whether commuter coordination can make the trip to campus more predictable. These are proposed features, not active services.

📡

Terminal Line Unblur Engine

A proposed terminal monitor for Apalit Junction and Bayan Terminal, showing estimated queue length and waiting time when commuter reports are available.

Planned • no live data yet
🤝

Instant Express Ride Pool

The concept groups up to 3 nearby LVCC batchmates to coordinate a direct express tricycle or jeep ride, with a proposed fare around ₱20 per seat.

Proposed • ₱20/seat
👑

Commuter Pro Pass

A proposed ₱99/month pass for priority express-ride matching, departure alerts before leaving home, and no platform fee on micro-transactions.

Proposed • ₱99/month
📲

QR & GCash Fare Split

A planned QR-based student/ride verification flow and shared fare settlement concept. No GCash integration or payments are active in this prototype.

Planned • not integrated
📶

Passive Mesh Telemetry

Your original Give-to-Get idea: opted-in commuter devices could contribute anonymous signal/queue observations in exchange for access to clearer commute information.

Concept • consent-first
🎟️

Batchmate Referral Reward

The original idea rewards inviting 2 campus batchmates with a free Express Pass. This is a proposed incentive, not an active referral program.

Proposed • invite 2

A few important things

Is FleetSignal already operating?

No. FleetSignal is currently a concept being tested. There are no live rides, live terminal metrics, or active commuter services.

Will joining cost anything?

No. Joining the waitlist is free. The prices shown are proposed prices for feedback only.

What happens after I sign up?

Your interest is recorded in this prototype. This standalone demo does not send emails or connect to a live database.
