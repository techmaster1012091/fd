The Commuter Challenge

Why LVCC Morning Commutes Need a Solution

LVCC Apalit students face recurring transit friction during morning rush hours.

Terminal Delays

Students spend significant time waiting in disorganized terminal queues during peak morning travel hours.

Unpredictable Wait Times

Lack of visibility into actual queue conditions makes planning morning departures unreliable.

Academic Impact

Commute friction contributes to morning fatigue and increases the risk of tardiness for early classes.

The Proposed Concept

Peer-to-Peer Express Matching Designed for Students

FleetSignal is a proposed crowd-coordination concept aimed at reducing terminal uncertainty and enabling students to coordinate express group rides.

  • Planned passive telemetry concept to monitor terminal density.
  • Proposed automated micro-fare splitting for group express rides.
  • Cryptographic QR-code handshake proposed for peer verification.
